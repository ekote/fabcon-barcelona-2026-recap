# D15. AI functions in a PySpark notebook

![GA](https://img.shields.io/badge/GA-1F7A4D?style=flat-square) Slide 27 · Recording: [English](../../en/media/S16-ai-functions.mp4) · [Polish](../../pl/media/S16-ai-functions.mp4) · [German](../../de/media/S16-ai-functions.mp4)

Translation, sentiment, and classification of tester comments in English, Arabic, and French, one line per AI function.

## You need

- The notebook `04_ai_functions` from `up`.
- The tenant setting **Users can use Copilot and other features powered by Azure OpenAI**, and a paid capacity of F2 or larger.

## Steps

1. Open the notebook **04_ai_functions**.
2. Select **Run all**.
3. Scroll to the result table.

## Expected result

Eight comments with a translation, a sentiment, and a topic from your labels, then `done: 8 comments`.

## Time and cost

About 4 minutes. Runs on the F8 capacity from `up`.
