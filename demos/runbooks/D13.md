# D13. OneLake catalog Govern

![GA](https://img.shields.io/badge/GA-1F7A4D?style=flat-square) Slide 18 · Recording: [English](../../en/media/S15-A19-catalog-govern.mp4) · [Polish](../../pl/media/S15-A19-catalog-govern.mp4) · [German](../../de/media/S15-A19-catalog-govern.mp4)

The admin view of the OneLake catalog: governance insights for the whole estate, domains, and recommended actions.

## You need

- The Fabric administrator role for the **All data in Fabric** view. Data owners see **My items**.

## Steps

1. Open **OneLake catalog** and select the **Govern** tab.
2. Read the insights. The report refreshes once a day, and the first open takes a few minutes.
3. Scroll to **Recommended actions**.

## Expected result

Governance insights, domains and subdomains, and recommended actions such as structuring data with domains.

## Time and cost

About 2 minutes. No Fabric capacity needed.
