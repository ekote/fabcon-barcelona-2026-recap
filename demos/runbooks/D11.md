# D11. Outbound access protection

![GA](https://img.shields.io/badge/GA-1F7A4D?style=flat-square) Slide 17 · Recording: [English](../../en/media/S14-A18-outbound-protection.mp4) · [Polish](../../pl/media/S14-A18-outbound-protection.mp4) · [German](../../de/media/S14-A18-outbound-protection.mp4)

A workspace setting that blocks outbound connections, so data cannot leave through shortcuts, notebooks, or data agents.

## You need

- The `WorkspaceBlockOutboundAccess` tenant setting, part of `settings on`, and the workspace Admin role.

## Steps

1. Open **Workspace settings** > **Outbound networking**.
2. Find **Outbound access protection** and **Block outbound public access**.
3. Look, do not switch it on in the demo workspace. It disables starter pools, blocks `pip install` from PyPI, and Osmos (D06) stops working there.

## Expected result

The setting and its allow list options: managed private endpoints and data connection rules.

## Time and cost

About 2 minutes. Runs on the F8 capacity from `up`.
