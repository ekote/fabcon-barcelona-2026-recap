# D17. Fabric data agent answers from a lakehouse

![GA](https://img.shields.io/badge/GA-1F7A4D?style=flat-square) Slide 29 · Recording: [English](../../en/media/S16-data-agent.mp4) · [Polish](../../pl/media/S16-data-agent.mp4) · [German](../../de/media/S16-data-agent.mp4)

A data agent turns a question into SQL on the lakehouse and answers in one sentence plus a table.

## You need

- The data agent from `up`: "Formula agent" in English, "Agent receptur" in Polish, "Rezeptur-Agent" in German.

## Steps

1. Open the data agent. The source **lh_oud_rd** shows on the left.
2. Ask the first question below and open the generated SQL.
3. Ask the second question.

## Prompts

**English**, prompt 1

```text
Which formula has the highest share of testers picking it over the reference, and what does a kilogram of its concentrate cost?
```

**English**, prompt 2

```text
Show a table of all formulas: name, average first-choice share against the reference, and cost per kilogram. Sort by share, highest first.
```

**Polish**, prompt 1

```text
Która receptura najczęściej wygrywa ze wzorcem i ile kosztuje kilogram jej koncentratu?
```

**Polish**, prompt 2

```text
Pokaż tabelę wszystkich receptur: nazwa, średni odsetek wyborów zamiast wzorca, koszt kilograma. Posortuj malejąco po odsetku.
```

**German**, prompt 1

```text
Welche Rezeptur hat den höchsten Anteil an Testpersonen, die sie der Referenz vorziehen, und was kostet ein Kilogramm ihres Konzentrats?
```

**German**, prompt 2

```text
Zeig mir eine Tabelle aller Rezepturen: Name, durchschnittlicher Anteil der Erstwahl gegenüber der Referenz und Kosten pro Kilogramm. Sortiert nach Anteil, höchster zuerst.
```

## Expected result

First answer: OB-104 Oud Saffron S-7, 68 percent, 14,353 QAR per kg. Second answer, sorted: Oud Saffron S-7 68, Oud Rose 57, Oud Daylight 53, Oud Plantation 52.5, Oud Amber 51, Oud Classic 48.

Output from the verification run on October 21, 2026, trimmed to the demo workspace, IDs replaced:

```text
Q: Which formula has the highest share of testers picking it over the reference, and what does a kilogram of its concentrate cost?

Oud Saffron S-7 (formula OB-104) has the highest tester preference with 68% first-choice share, and its concentrate costs 14,353 QAR per kilogram.

Response time: 18 sec
```

## Time and cost

About 4 minutes. Runs on the F8 capacity from `up`.
