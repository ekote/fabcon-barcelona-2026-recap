# D10. OneLake security roles

![GA](https://img.shields.io/badge/GA-1F7A4D?style=flat-square) Slide 17 · Recording: [English](../../en/media/S14-A16-onelake-security.mp4) · [Polish](../../pl/media/S14-A16-onelake-security.mp4) · [German](../../de/media/S14-A16-onelake-security.mp4)

OneLake security on a lakehouse: roles grant Read on tables or folders, with row and column rules, and every lakehouse starts with a DefaultReader role.

## You need

- The lakehouse `lh_oud_rd` from `up`, and the Admin or Member workspace role.

## Steps

1. Open **lh_oud_rd** and select **Manage OneLake security**.
2. Open the **DefaultReader** role. It grants **Read** to everyone with the ReadAll permission.
3. Check **Members**. Before you add a restricted role, remove its users from DefaultReader, or they keep full access.

## Expected result

The role list with **DefaultReader**, its Read permission, and its virtual members.

## Time and cost

About 3 minutes. Runs on the F8 capacity from `up`.
